CLASSROOM PROTOTYPE

One station, three safety gates

Sensitizer is a classroom prototype for exploring how a hygiene station could communicate hand presence, sanitizer supply and spill conditions to the people who maintain it.

Unit labelSENS-01
PlacementNorth lab · wall mount
Prototype firmwaredemo 0.3.2
Data modeLocal sample layer

ⓘ What is connected?

Nothing in this frontend is connected to classroom hardware. Readings, events and alert examples are supplied values for reviewing the interface and operating thresholds.
